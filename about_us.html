ABOUT US

The first leading global bottling company, PolySite was founded twenty years ago. We specialise in the manufacture, supply, packaging, and export of a variety of PET, PP, and PC bottles for several industries including pharmaceuticals, chemicals, water, liquor, and more. We provide heat-resistant, durable, recyclable, and eco-friendly bottles for everyday use.

PolySite is a recognised member of the Standard Organisation of Nigeria (SON) and the National Food and Drug Administration and Control (NAFDAC). We have also partnered with several foreign agencies including the National Medical Products Administration (NMPA) and the United States Food and Drug Administration (USFDA/FDA). PolySite has been a supplier to its home country and to over 40 countries and industries around the world. We also offer custom packaging solutions. Trust us to bring you the best packaging solutions.

OUR HISTORY

A look at our growth and achievemets over the years

2000
Company Founded

PolySite was established in Nigeria with 10 dedicated members and essential manufacturing equipment.

2002
ISO 9001 Certification

PolySite achieved its ISO 9001 Certification — a major quality milestone.

2004
Product Launch

The manufacturing of PET, PP, and PC bottles began and we started supplying to local markets in Nigeria.

2005
Expansion to New Markets

PolySite expanded its operations to include new regional markets.

2007
Supply and Exportation Grew in High Demand

PolySite was able to meet the increasing demand for its products both locally and internationally.

2009
Acquisition of New Technology

PolySite invested in new technology to enhance its manufacturing capabilities.

2010
Custom Packaging began

PolySite started developing personalized packaging solutions for individuals and industries worldwide.

2011
Agencies Partnership

PolySite established partnerships with several foreign agencies to expand its reach and capabilities.

2013
Wide Export Growth

Supply and exportation grew significantly,reaching markets across multiple continents worldwide.

2016
China Branch Opened

A new branch of PolySite was established in China, strengthening our global presence.

2018 - Present
Global Presence Established

PolySite now serves over 40 countries worldwide, supplying industries and individuals with quality packaging solutions.

OUR MISSION, VISION AND VALUES

Our Mission

Our Vision

To satisfy individuals and industries with our high quality packaging solutions, and to contribute to a cleaner environment through sustainable and recyclable manufacturing practices.
